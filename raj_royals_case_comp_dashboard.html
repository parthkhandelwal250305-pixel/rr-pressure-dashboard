How to read this (one minute)
Pressure ball
A delivery in the top 25% of all deliveries by how much the next ball can move the batting side’s chance of winning. 46,709 such deliveries in 781 matches.
Rating (θ)
Runs’ worth a player adds per 100 pressure balls compared with an average player in the same situations. Higher is better for batters and bowlers alike. 0 = average.
± SD
How unsure the rating is. A player with 20 ± 8 could plausibly be anywhere from about 4 to 36.
Top 10 in N of 500
I re-ran the rating 500 times on resampled matches. This counts how often the player stayed in the top ten. ★ stable core = 300 or more.
†
Thin exposure: fewer than 20 matches with pressure balls.
Rank
Among the 137 batters or 151 bowlers with at least 100 pressure balls in that role.
“All balls”
Ordinary strike rate or economy over every ball in the analysed matches. Descriptive only: it shows where and how a player is used. The rating is what ranks players.

What the lists say, in three lines

  • Six names on each list (★) stay in the top ten in at least 300 of 500 re-runs. The last four places on each list are soft: the players just outside are close behind.
  • Of the twenty, only Wanindu Hasaranga was released for the IPL 2027 auction. The other nineteen were kept by their franchises.
  • The ratings rank who is best in high-stakes moments. They do not claim a player lifts his game above his own usual level under pressure: that difference is too small to measure in about 100 pressure balls.
